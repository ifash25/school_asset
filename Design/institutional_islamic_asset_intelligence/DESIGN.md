## Brand & Style

This design system serves an institutional Islamic educational foundation managing extensive physical assets, buildings, land, and digital inventories across multi-tiered educational units (from Tahfizh/RQ through SMK and central foundation levels).

### Aesthetic Direction: Dignified Institutional Precision
The aesthetic blends the solemnity, trust, and permanence of an established educational endowment (Waqf & Yayasan) with the razor-sharp functional clarity of enterprise SaaS. It rejects ornamental cliches in favor of understated dignity:
- **Structural Architecture:** High-density, data-first grids reminiscent of financial auditing platforms and sovereign registries.
- **Atmosphere:** Clean, daylight-oriented canvases layered over subtle warm parchment underpinnings, grounded by deep emerald tones rather than tech-generic cobalt blue.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Impeccable stewardship (*amanah*), audit-grade order, long-term permanence, and contemporary administrative agility.

## Colors

The palette balances administrative authority, operational signaling, and warm archival legibility.

### Primary & Brand Identity
- **Primary Institutional Emerald (`#0F5132`):** Primary command elements, top-level navigation states, active unit switches, and formal actions. Represents stewardship, institutional stability, and foundation identity.
- **Forest Accent (`#157347`):** Interactive hover states for primary buttons, deep-fill active badges, and confirmation accents.

### Supporting & Functional Roles
- **Warm Parchment Surface (`#F9F8F5`):** Serves as an institutional backdrop layer behind pure white data cards, softening high-density fatigue while nodding to educational manuscripts.
- **Secondary Amber / Gold (`#D97706`):** Reserved for items under maintenance, depreciation milestones, and warning notifications.
- **Tech Teal / Cyan (`#0D9488`):** Allocated to audit cycles, analytics telemetry, inventory barcoding triggers, and verified ledger states.
- **Disposal / Damaged Crimson (`#DC2626`):** Dedicated exclusively to de-accessioning, critical write-offs, broken assets, and urgent audit discrepancies.

### Neutral Scale
- **Canvas Base:** Crisp Slate (`#F8FAFC`) on top-level content canvases; Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated cards, modals, and dense table sheets.
- **Borders & Dividers:** High-clarity subtle zinc lines (`#E2E8F0` and `#CBD5E1`) for strict data separation without chromatic distraction.
- **Typography Neutrals:** Slate Deep (`#0F172A`) for titles, Slate Body (`#334155`) for secondary values, and Muted Slate (`#64748B`) for micro-labels and table headers.

## Typography

The typographic system utilizes a paired dual-engine approach to distinguish institutional page-level framing from intensive transactional data processing.

- **Primary Display & Headings:** `Plus Jakarta Sans` provides geometric friendliness with an architectural presence. It offers balanced roundness without being juvenile, grounding page titles, statistic counters, and high-level card headers.
- **Dense Data & UI Copy:** `Inter` is deployed across all data tables, inputs, sidebars, modal structures, and metadata tags. Its tall x-height and neutral geometric apertures guarantee legible rendering of Indonesian naming conventions, serial numbers (SKU/NUP), monetary asset values, and status chips even at dense 11px/12px scales.
- **Numeric & Asset Code Conventions:** Asset identifiers (e.g., `YAY-2024-SD-INV-0042`) and currency valuations must utilize tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to preserve spatial alignment across multi-column enterprise tables.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined, non-negotiable 8px base rhythm (with 4px sub-increments for micro badges and tight table rows) underpins the workspace.

### Enterprise Layout Engine
- **Canvas Model:** 12-column dynamic desktop fluid grid encased within a master application shell. Side navigation collapses between 260px (expanded) and 72px (iconic unit selector).
- **Desktop (>= 1280px):** 24px column gutters and 32px canvas margins. Multi-column metric panels, side-by-side asset comparison drawers, and full-width data tables.
- **Tablet / Small Workstations (768px - 1279px):** 16px column gutters and 24px margins. Two-tier metric stacking; table headers gain horizontal scrolling pinned on left-most Asset ID.
- **Field Inspector Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked stream. 16px margins. Tables convert to discrete high-density key-value cards optimized for barcode scanning and on-site room-by-room asset audits.

### Vertical Spatial Rhythm
Component internals follow a compact enterprise cadence:
- **Stat Cards & Metrics:** 16px inner padding.
- **Table Cells:** 10px vertical cell padding for normal view, 6px vertical for dense audit mode.
- **Filter Bars & Search Strips:** 12px outer spacing with 8px internal gaps between badge filters.

## Elevation & Depth

To avoid visual fatigue in data-heavy screens, elevation relies primarily on crisp perimeter delineation reinforced by whispered, tinted shadows.

### Atmospheric Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Flat Canvas / Foundation Bed):** `#F8FAFC` slate backdrop or `#F9F8F5` warm sand workspace floor. No shadow, zero elevation.
2. **Level 1 (Structural Cards & Tables):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface bounded by a razor-thin 1px solid border (`#E2E8F0`). Shadow: `0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
3. **Level 2 (Hover States, Active Filter Popovers, Dropdowns):** `#FFFFFF` surface. Border: `#CBD5E1`. Shadow: `0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
4. **Level 3 (Asset Detail Flyout Drawers & Audit Modals):** `#FFFFFF` surface. Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`. Accompanied by a semi-opaque dimming overlay (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(2px)`).

### Tonal Surface Anchoring
Sticky table headers and bulk action floating toolbars utilize `#FFFFFF` with `95%` opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`, and a defined bottom boundary border (`#E2E8F0`) to maintain orientation while scrolling extensive records.

## Shapes

The shape system employs disciplined, professional curvature that balances modern SaaS interfaces with traditional institutional decorum.

### Radius Assignments
- **Micro Radius (4px - 6px):** Form inputs, checkboxes, table cell indicator chips, and contextual tooltips.
- **Component Standard (8px):** Primary and secondary buttons, filter buttons, stat summary containers, dropdown menus, and standard asset item cards.
- **Container / Boundary (12px):** Master data table shells, summary dashboard cards, multi-unit filter pillbars, modal dialogs, and barcode inspection viewports.
- **Pill / Circular (9999px):** Strictly constrained to institutional unit identification badges (e.g., `TK`, `SD`, `Yayasan`) and numeric counter badges to create instant visual differentiation against rectangular enterprise data fields.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Deep Emerald Forest (`#0F5132`) background, crisp white typography, 8px border radius, 36px default height (32px dense). Hover transitions to `#157347`. Focus ring: 2px offset with `#0F5132` at 30% alpha.
- **Secondary / Institutional Outline:** White surface with 1px border (`#CBD5E1`), text `#1E293B`. Hover shifts to `#F8FAFC` background with emerald-tinted border (`#0F5132`).
- **Destructive:** Crisp Crimson outline or soft wash (`#FEF2F2` background, `#DC2626` text, `#FCA5A5` border) for asset disposal or liquidation actions.

### Educational Unit Badges
A custom signature component providing quick visual filtering across multi-tier foundation branches:
- **Yayasan (Central Foundation):** Deep Emerald wash (`#ECFDF5` background, `#065F46` text, `#A7F3D0` border).
- **RQ (Rumah Qur'an / Tahfizh):** Warm Sand & Gold tint (`#FEF3C7` background, `#92400E` text, `#FDE68A` border).
- **TK (Taman Kanak-Kanak):** Soft Amber (`#FFFBEB` background, `#B45309` text, `#FCD34D` border).
- **SD (Sekolah Dasar):** Tech Teal (`#F0FDFA` background, `#115E59` text, `#99F6E4` border).
- **SMP:** Indigo Slate (`#EEF2FF` background, `#3730A3` text, `#C7D2FE` border).
- **SMK:** Professional Blue (`#F0F9FF` background, `#075985` text, `#BAE6FD` border).
All badges rendered with uppercase label-sm typography and 9999px pill radius.

### Input Fields & Search Filters
- **Form Controls:** 36px height, 8px radius, white background, `#CBD5E1` border. Active focus triggers an Emerald stroke (`#0F5132`) with a smooth 0.2s 3px focus glow (`rgba(15, 81, 50, 0.12)`).
- **Prefix Icons & Asset Code Scanner:** Left-aligned 16px iconography (e.g., QR/Barcode or magnifying glass) colored in `#64748B`.

### Data Tables & High-Density Grids
- **Header Structure:** 40px height, `#F8FAFC` background, uppercase 11px label typography with subtle letter-spacing, bottom border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Row Architecture:** Alternating or clean hoverable rows (`#FFFFFF` resting, `#F9F8F5` hover), 44px standard height (36px high-density). Bottom cell border `#F1F5F9`.
- **Numeric & Valuation Alignment:** Right-aligned monetary values (Rupiah / IDR) set in `Inter` with tabular figures for instant visual auditing.

### Asset Condition Status Indicators
- **Baik (Good):** Dot indicator `#157347` inside soft emerald badge.
- **Perlu Perbaikan (Maintenance):** Dot indicator `#D97706` inside warm amber badge.
- **Rusak Berat / Dihapus (Damaged/Disposed):** Dot indicator `#DC2626` inside clean crimson badge.

### Stat Cards (Ringkasan Aset)
- Pure white background, 12px roundedness, 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
- Features a top-aligned 3px accent stroke: Emerald for total active capital value, Teal for audited inventory, Amber for maintenance requests, and Crimson for depreciated write-offs.
